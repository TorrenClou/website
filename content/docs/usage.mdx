## Adding & Analyzing Torrents

TorrentDrive uses a two-step **Upload & Review** workflow so you can inspect swarm health and select
individual files before any transfer begins:

1. Click **Upload Torrent** in the sidebar (`/torrents/upload`).
2. Drag and drop one or more `.torrent` files (up to 10 MB each).
3. TorrentDrive parses the `.torrent` metadata and scrapes both **UDP (BEP 15)** and **HTTP/HTTPS (BEP 48)**
   trackers in parallel to compute a live **Health Score**, seeder count, and leecher count.
   - If a torrent has the **BEP 27 Private Flag** (`private=1`), TorrentDrive displays a **Private** badge
     and strictly isolates it to its embedded tracker URLs — never querying public fallback trackers,
     DHT, PEX, or Local Peer Discovery.
4. On the **Review Torrents** screen (`/torrents/analyze`):
   - Expand any torrent in the batch to **select or deselect individual files**. Unselected files are
     skipped, and shared boundary pieces are isolated in hidden partial files so only your chosen
     files are uploaded to the cloud.
   - Choose a **Save to Storage** profile for the entire batch, or override the destination on
     individual torrents.
   - If tracker scraping reports `0` seeders on a swarm that actually has peers via DHT/PEX, you can
     still **Force Start** the transfer.
5. Click **Start download** (or **Start all N downloads**).

## Monitoring Transfers

Open **Dashboard** (`/dashboard`) or **Jobs** (`/jobs`) to watch transfers in real time:

- **Status Progression**: `QUEUED` → `DOWNLOADING` → `PENDING_UPLOAD` → `UPLOADING` → `COMPLETED`
- **Dual-Phase Telemetry**: Every job card displays side-by-side progress for the **Download Phase**
  (BitTorrent pieces fetched, live `↓ MB/s`, and ETA) and the **Upload Phase** (cloud bytes transferred,
  live `↑ MB/s`, ETA, and target Storage Profile), plus an `InfoHash` copy chip, file selection summary,
  and a step-by-step **Job Timeline** on `/jobs/<id>`.
- **Inline Quick Actions**: Trigger **Force Start**, **Retry**, or **Cancel** directly from any job card
  on `/dashboard` or `/jobs`, or use **Retry All Failed** and multi-select batch actions to recover or
  cancel multiple transfers at once.

## Searching, Filtering & Sorting Jobs

The **Jobs Command Console** (`/jobs`) provides server-side search, multi-facet filtering, and sorting
across your entire transfer history with shareable URL query parameters:

- **Token-Aware Search (`/` shortcut)**: Search across torrent filenames, inner file paths, `InfoHash`,
  Storage Profile names, error messages, or job IDs (`#42`). Combine free text with structured query tokens:
  - `provider:gdrive` or `provider:s3` — filter by Storage Provider
  - `phase:active`, `phase:downloading`, `phase:uploading`, `phase:retrying`, `phase:failed` — filter by lifecycle phase
  - `is:pinned`, `is:rerouted`, `is:stalled`, `is:selective`, `has:error` — filter by routing or health traits
  - `type:video`, `type:audio`, `type:archive`, `type:document`, `type:software` — filter by media category
  - `size:<1gb`, `size:1-10gb`, `size:10-50gb`, `size:>50gb` — filter by transfer size bucket
- **Facet Dropdowns & Smart Views**: Filter by specific Storage Profile, media category, size bucket, or
  creation date range (`Today`, `Last 7 Days`, `Last 30 Days`, `Last 90 Days`), or apply one-click
  **Smart Views** (such as *Needs Attention*, *Active Cloud Uploads*, *Rerouted Transfers*, or *Large Media*).
- **Multi-Field Sorting**: Sort transfers ascending or descending by **Created Date**, **Total Size**,
  **Transfer Progress**, **Live Speed**, **File Name**, or **Completed Date**.

## Managing Destinations, Pinning & Failover

Every job detail page (`/jobs/<id>`) includes a **Destination** card:

- **Switch Drive Mid-Job**: While a job is queued, downloading, or waiting to upload, you can move
  it to any other healthy storage profile using **Send to another drive**.
- **Automatic Failover**: By default, if a destination drive runs out of quota, loses authorization,
  or hits consecutive upload failures (`FailureThreshold`), TorrentDrive automatically reroutes the
  upload to another healthy storage profile (`FailoverQuotaExceeded`, `FailoverNeedsReauth`,
  `FailoverUnhealthy`).
- **Pin to this drive**: Turn on **Pin to this drive** on the Destination card if you want the job
  to stay on its assigned drive and fail rather than rerouting elsewhere.

## Local Disk Cleanup & Purge

Under **Settings** (`/dashboard/settings`):

- **Delete downloads after a successful upload**: Enabled by default. Deletes `/data/downloads/<jobId>`
  as soon as every selected file is confirmed in your cloud storage.
- **Local Storage & Purge**: Scans `/data/downloads` and breaks usage down into **Can be freed**
  (completed or cancelled jobs), **In use** (active or retrying jobs), and **Unrecognised**
  directories. Click **Purge** to reclaim disk space from completed or cancelled jobs at any time.

## Common Container Operations

```bash
# Follow live container logs
docker logs -f torrentdrive

# Check all 9 internal supervisord processes
docker exec torrentdrive supervisorctl status

# Restart the container (all data & queued jobs persist)
docker restart torrentdrive
```
