title: Google Drive

## Prerequisites

A Google Cloud project with the **Google Drive API** enabled.

## Step 1: Create OAuth Credentials

1. Open [Google Cloud Console](https://console.cloud.google.com/) → **APIs & Services** → **Credentials**
2. Click **Create Credentials** → **OAuth client ID**
3. Application type: **Web application**
4. Add the **Authorized redirect URI** shown in the TorrentDrive Google Drive form:
   - **Local**: `http://localhost:47100/proxy/api/storage/gdrive/callback`
   - **Custom domain / Reverse proxy**: `https://<your-domain>/proxy/api/storage/gdrive/callback`
5. Copy the **Client ID** and **Client Secret**

## Step 2: Connect Your Account

1. In TorrentDrive, go to **Storage** → **Add Storage Profile** → **Google Drive** (or complete Step 2 of the first-run setup wizard)
2. Save your **Client ID** and **Client Secret**
3. Click **Connect Google Account** and complete the Google OAuth consent screen in the popup window
4. Give the profile a name, optionally set a target folder ID, and mark it as your **Default** profile if desired

## Health Probes & Automatic Failover

TorrentDrive periodically checks each Google Drive profile's token validity and free quota:

- Healthy profiles display a **Healthy** badge and remaining free storage.
- If a Google Drive account runs out of storage or its refresh token is revoked, TorrentDrive marks the
  profile as **Quota Exceeded** or **Needs Re-auth** and automatically reroutes active uploads to
  another healthy storage profile (unless the job is pinned to that drive).

## Data Privacy & Security

- OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are stored inside your container's PostgreSQL database on the
  `torrentdrive-pgdata` volume — protect access to the host volume as described in [Security](/docs/security).
- Files stream directly from your server to Google Drive using resumable upload sessions.
- To revoke access at any time, visit [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
